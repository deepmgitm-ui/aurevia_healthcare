The process
Step 01 · Talk ~ 15 minutes

A free, honest first conversation.

Share your reports over a phone call or WhatsApp. Your coordinator listens, asks the right questions, and frames your options honestly — even if that means telling you a procedure may not be needed yet.

What we ask

Symptoms, history, prior consultations, current medications and what's worrying you most. No medical jargon.

What we share

The range of treatment options, what each one involves, and what a realistic timeline and cost would look like.

Privacy

Your reports stay with our care team. We never share them with hospitals before your explicit consent.

No pressure

If you decide it's not the right time, we close the file. No follow-up sales calls. Ever.

Step 02 · Match ~ 24 hours

The right specialist. The right hospital.

We match you with a senior surgeon and a facility chosen for your specific condition — never the highest commission. Our network is curated for outcomes, not advertising.

Senior specialists

Every doctor we work with has 10+ years of experience in their specialty. Reputed, not just well-marketed.

Right hospital

We pick the facility that fits your procedure, location and insurance — not a flat "preferred partner" list.

Second opinion

For major procedures we arrange an independent second opinion before any final decision.

Patient fit

Bedside manner, language, hospital ambience and family-friendliness all factor into who we recommend.

Step 03 · Plan ~ 48–72 hours

Written estimate. Cashless approval.

Procedure, costs, timelines and recovery plan — in writing. We coordinate insurance pre-approvals before admission, so you never receive a surprise bill at discharge.

Itemised estimate

Surgeon's fee, hospital, anaesthesia, room category, implants, medications — line by line. No vague totals.

Cashless coverage

100% cashless across major Indian health insurers. We file paperwork and get pre-authorisation before admission.

Scheduling

Procedure dates, pre-op tests, dietary instructions and family logistics — all booked and confirmed in one go.

Recovery plan

You see the expected post-op timeline upfront — time off work, garments, follow-ups, restrictions.

Step 04 · Heal Through full recovery

Treatment, discharge, follow-through.

Admission, surgery, discharge and follow-up visits — handled. Your coordinator stays reachable through recovery and beyond, for every question, every report, every concerned family member.

Day of admission

We meet you at the hospital, handle paperwork, and brief your family on the timeline.

Post-op

Discharge summary explained, prescriptions arranged, follow-up visits booked. We translate the medical-speak.

Always reachable

WhatsApp, phone or in-person — Monday to Saturday, 9 to 9. Real humans, not chatbots.

Beyond

For complex procedures we stay in touch for months. Recovery isn't a deadline — it's a relationship.

What to expect.

A quick scan of how the experience feels in practice — what we ask of you, what you can ask of us, and where it ends.

Common questions.

If your question isn't here, ask us directly — info@aureviahealthcare.com or via WhatsApp.

/ 01Is the initial consultation actually free?
Yes — the first consultation with our care team is completely free, and so is the second opinion we arrange before any major procedure. You only pay if and when you choose to proceed with treatment.
/ 02How is Aurevia different from a hospital's helpline?
A hospital's helpline is naturally trying to bring you into that hospital. We are independent — we recommend the specialist and facility that fit your condition, even if that's a different hospital than the one closest to us. Outcomes first, always.
/ 03Do you take a commission from the hospital?
We're a healthcare facilitation platform — fee structures are transparent and never come at the cost of your recommendation. We tell every patient honestly when a procedure isn't needed, and that policy has never changed.
/ 04Will my insurance really be cashless?
Yes — for any procedure covered under your policy, we file the cashless paperwork and obtain pre-authorisation before admission. You don't pay out of pocket and chase reimbursement later.
/ 05What if I'm not based in Delhi NCR or Mumbai?
Many of our patients travel from other cities for specific specialists. We coordinate stay, transport, pre-op tests and follow-up reports digitally — so out-of-town doesn't mean out of touch.
/ 06Can I choose my own surgeon?
Absolutely. If you already have a doctor you trust, we can coordinate care around them. If you don't, we'll match you to one of our senior specialists — and you can change your mind at any point before treatment.
/ 07How quickly can a procedure be scheduled?
Routine procedures are usually scheduled within a week or two. Urgent conditions are prioritised — we've coordinated same-day specialist consultations and admissions when the situation needed it.

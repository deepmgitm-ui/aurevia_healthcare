/ 01 — Eye care

LASIK treatment

LASIK is an advanced laser eye surgery used to correct nearsightedness, farsightedness and astigmatism by reshaping the cornea. It is one of the safest and most effective vision correction procedures — quick, minimally invasive, and offering long-lasting visual improvement with a fast recovery period.

Duration10–15 min per eye
AnaesthesiaNumbing eye drops
StayDay-care · same day
Recovery24–48 hours
Success rateHighly accurate
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

Numbing drops are applied, a femtosecond laser creates a thin corneal flap, and an excimer laser reshapes the underlying tissue. The flap is then repositioned and heals naturally — no stitches.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Freedom from glasses & contacts
  • Rapid visual recovery
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Excellent long-term accuracy
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Age 18+ with stable prescription
  • Healthy eyes & adequate cornea
  • No cataract, glaucoma or severe dry eye
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Most patients see clearly within 24 hours. Mild dryness or light sensitivity for a few days. Avoid eye-rubbing, swimming and contact sports for 1–2 weeks.

/ 02 — Eye care

Cataract surgery

A safe and effective procedure for cataracts — a clouding of the eye's natural lens that blurs vision. The cloudy lens is removed and replaced with a clear intraocular lens (IOL), restoring clarity and dramatically improving quality of life.

Duration10–15 min
TechniqueMics/Flacs
StaySame-day discharge
Recovery1-2 weeks
Success rate99.9%+
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

A 2–3 mm incision; ultrasound (phacoemulsification) breaks up the cloudy lens; a foldable IOL is implanted through the same incision and settles into position.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Clearer, brighter vision
  • Better colour perception
  • Reduced glare at night
  • Independence in daily life
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Blurred or cloudy vision
  • Difficulty reading or driving
  • Light sensitivity & glare
  • Frequent prescription changes
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Vision improves within days. Use prescribed drops, wear an eye shield while sleeping for a week, and avoid heavy lifting. Stable vision in 4–6 weeks.

/ 03 — ENT

Sinus treatment

Treatment for sinus infections and chronic sinusitis — congestion, headaches, facial pain and disturbed sleep. Depending on severity, we offer medications, lifestyle plans, or minimally invasive procedures such as FESS or balloon sinuplasty.

ApproachFESS / balloon sinuplasty
IncisionsNone — through nose
StayDay-care
RecoveryFew days to 2 weeks
ForChronic sinusitis 12+ wks
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

Mild cases — medication, sprays and steam. Severe cases — a thin endoscope is passed through the nose; blocked passages are cleared, polyps removed or a small balloon inflated to open them.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Relief from congestion & pain
  • Better breathing & sleep
  • Reduced sinus infections
  • Improved sense of smell
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Chronic nasal blockage
  • Recurring infections / headaches
  • Snoring / disturbed sleep
  • No relief from medication alone
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Mild swelling and congestion for a few days; saline sprays and medication support healing. Most return to normal activity within days; complete healing in a few weeks.

/ 04 — ENT

Tonsillectomy

Surgical removal of the tonsils for people who suffer frequent tonsil infections, breathing difficulty or enlarged tonsils that disrupt daily life. We use modern coblation technique — less bleeding, less pain, faster recovery than traditional methods.

Duration30–45 minutes
AnaesthesiaGeneral
TechniqueCoblation / laser
StaySame-day discharge
Recovery10–14 days
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

Performed through the mouth — no external cuts or scars. Tonsils are removed using coblation, electrocautery or laser. Same-day discharge after a short observation period.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Far fewer throat infections
  • Relief from chronic sore throat
  • Better breathing & sleep
  • Reduced snoring
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Frequent / chronic tonsillitis
  • Enlarged tonsils, sleep apnea
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Persistent bad breath
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Cold, soft foods (ice cream, smoothies); stay hydrated. Pain peaks around days 5–7; full recovery in 10–14 days. Prescribed pain relief and antibiotics support healing.

/ 05 — Cosmetic surgery

Liposuction

A cosmetic procedure to remove stubborn fat from the abdomen, thighs, hips, arms, back or chin. It is a body-contouring tool — not a weight-loss surgery — designed to refine areas that don't respond to diet and exercise.

AnaesthesiaLocal / general
TechniquesTumescent · laser · USG-assisted
StayDay-care typically
GarmentCompression wear
ResultsLong-lasting
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

A tumescent solution loosens fat through small incisions; a thin cannula gently suctions it out. Advanced laser- or ultrasound-assisted techniques may be used for smoother, more precise results.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Improved body contour
  • Removed fat cells gone for good
  • Better clothing fit
  • Long-lasting results
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Within 30% of ideal weight
  • Good skin elasticity
  • Stubborn fat unchanged by diet
  • Healthy & realistic expectations
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Mild swelling and bruising for a few days. Compression garments support healing. Light activity within days; normal routine in a few weeks. Healthy lifestyle maintains the result.

/ 06 — General surgery

Lipoma removal

A minor surgical procedure to remove a lipoma — a soft, non-cancerous fatty lump beneath the skin. Lipomas are usually harmless but can cause discomfort, restrict movement, or be a cosmetic concern depending on size and location.

DurationShort procedure
AnaesthesiaLocal
StaySame-day discharge
StitchesRemoved in 7–14 days
RecurrenceVery low
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

A small incision is made over the lipoma; the fatty tissue and its capsule are removed completely. Larger lipomas may use liposuction-assisted removal. Tissue is sent to a lab to confirm its benign nature.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Removal of fatty lump
  • Relief from pain / restriction
  • Cosmetic improvement
  • Low recurrence risk
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Visible / growing lump
  • Pain or restricted movement
  • Cosmetic concerns
  • Diagnosis needs confirming
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Mild swelling and soreness for a few days. Light activity quickly; avoid strenuous work until healing is complete. Stitches removed in 7–14 days; scars fade over 3–6 months.

/ 07 — Cosmetic + ENT

Rhinoplasty

Nose reshaping surgery — cosmetic, functional, or both. Rhinoplasty can refine the size, angle, bridge, tip or nostrils, and correct breathing issues caused by a deviated septum. Done well, the result still feels like you, just balanced.

AnaesthesiaLocal / general
TechniquesOpen · closed
StayDay-care / short stay
Back to work2–3 weeks
Final resultUp to 1 year
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

Bone, cartilage and soft tissue are reshaped through an open technique (small incision between nostrils) or closed technique (all incisions inside the nose). Usually a few hours; same-day or short stay.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Improved shape & symmetry
  • Better breathing
  • Correction of injury / birth defects
  • Natural, long-lasting results
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Unhappy with nose shape
  • Breathing difficulty
  • Past nasal injury
  • Adult, healthy, realistic
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Splint off at 1 week; visible improvement immediately. Most swelling subsides by 2–3 weeks. Final refined result over the following months as subtle swelling resolves.

/ 09 — Urology

Kidney stone treatment

Removal or breakdown of hard mineral deposits that form inside the kidneys — causing severe pain, blood in urine, and infection if left untreated. Small stones may pass naturally; larger ones need ESWL, URS or PCNL.

ApproachesESWL · URS · PCNL
AnaesthesiaLocal / spinal / general
StayDay-care to 2 days
RecoveryDays for ESWL/URS
ForStones 5 mm+
Book free consultation →
A · Procedure
How it works

The right technique for the right stone. ESWL uses sound waves to break stones externally. URS passes a scope through the urinary tract to break and remove them. PCNL is a small-incision keyhole surgery for very large stones.

B · Benefits
What you gain
  • Relief from severe pain
  • Restored urine flow
  • Reduced infection risk
  • Protected kidney function
C · Eligibility
Right for you if
  • Severe back / abdominal pain
  • Blood in urine
  • Stones that won't pass
  • Recurrent UTIs
D · Recovery
After the procedure

Most patients return to normal activity within days. Mild burning or blood-tinged urine settles quickly. Hydration and dietary plans reduce the risk of new stones forming.
